# Changelog

## 1.0.0 - 2026-11-01

First public release.

- Three processing methods: Spark, Blaze and Pulse, with integrity checks.
- MOV to MP4 conversion without re-encoding and optional compression.
- `/analyze` and `/check` (SSIM and VMAF comparison against the published video).
- Telegram Stars payments with automatic refunds, refund recovery after restarts, and an optional 30-day subscription.
- Per-user job locks with heartbeat, single-instance lock on PostgreSQL, and optional owner log chat.
- Iraqi Arabic and English interface.
